Two-Sentence Pitch:

Island Off Outer Darkness is an Interactive Fiction Walking Simulator ("Narrative Delivery Simulator") where you deliver packages and talk to people over the course of half a year in-game (as well as fish and garden). IOOD has recieved significant praise for its realistic character writing and complex art.

Description:

Island Off Outer Darkness follows the narrative of the Postman, a resident Postal Worker new to a small fishing village in Antarctic waters off the coast of a volcano (the titular Outer Darkness). They start new to the job, and must deliver packages to residents day-by-day, week-by-week. As they learn more about the island and its townsfolk, aberrations in space begin to appear. More broadly, Island Off Outer Darkness is an exploration of the grief of community collapse and personal withering. Featuring a 31-track OST by CalamityRhapsody and visual artistic work by several people, Island Off Outer Darkness is the story of this town, and these people.

Ethos:

Island Off Outer Darkness was built in the cooling aftermath of the withering and destruction of Rowdy Fuckers Cop Killers' ENDLESS WAR, a MUD that I was a member of and developed for and community managed and administrated. From that, partly as an exorcism, partly as autofiction, partly as the desire to tell a story about RFCK, and its people, Island Off Outer Darkness was gruelingly crafted through an arduous and tumultuous year-and-some-change development period (as well as about 6 months of post-launch support to polish it up). The end result is a dense, long, slow, atmospheric, difficult, hard-to-describe work which many people have enjoyed greatly. I, Zug, struggle talking about it, because it's so all-consuming and... "exorcised", that I don't even know how to scope it in my mind. It's the story of everything, to me, and I hope I captured it, and I think I did in part.
