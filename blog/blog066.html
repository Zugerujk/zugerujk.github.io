Just shaved. I don't do so very often. I find it very difficult. I've never been comfortable with my face ever since I started growing facial hair — both with and without. I joke that I wish I could reroll the cancer gacha for my facial hair — chemo often completely changes how your head and facial grows — and, idk. I don't like mine. It doesn't feel right.
