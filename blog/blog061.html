I played IMMORTALITY

I was playing pool a few weeks ago, at Minneapolis College's communal pool/ping pong area. It was a Thursday. It was me, Sam, and then 3 random guys. The three randoms walked in as I was explaining to Sam how I was gay and non-binary. It was awkward. And I always have to explain these things to people. It feels awful.

One of the three guys was very weird about it. Kept asking me political questions. Everybody else left after a bit and then it was just me and him. He asked me whether he'd be gay if he was into me. He made me feel viscerally uncomfortable in a way I had never before.

It made me feel like an other. It made me feel like an object. It made me feel like a male. It made me feel like I wasn't even there. Like I was male before I was non-binary, the latter being some added context. That I was illicit. He said he'd see me next Thursday. I don't go to the pool table on Thursday anymore.

That was a while back. I was on the second floor of the main campus this morning (or, rather, last) after a math exam. I think I did mediocre. And all of a sudden he was in line behind me, at the coffee shop. I went and sat down and then he walked away. We happened to be in the same place at the same time. No big deal. Nothing happened.

I feel like I'm not even there sometimes. I'm playing the part of someone who would have reactions to these sorts of things. I exist in a society where I am male, and that's the person I will forever be to everyone who never knows me. And I am going to live and die in this society. I will not see another world.

I played IMMORTALITY last week. It's really very good. I wish I had more to say, but it's one of the games I've played where you just have to play it. I played Lain PSX before it, and IMMORTALITY does a much better job of anything Lain PSX ever tried. It made me feel undeveloped as a writer.
