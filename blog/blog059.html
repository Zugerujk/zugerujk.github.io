THE BASED ONES Minecraft 2024

Last month, I played a shitton of modded Minecraft with a bunch of fellas from the Based 111s, an RFCK offshoot friend Discord server, owned by V. It was real fun! The primary characters were me, Liz, Dema, V, and SolarMoth, though Soba and Heavy/Light were there too (I think? My memory's bad). One of the main mods was that the player chose an "origin" (which I think is terribly balanced), but I chose the origin that makes you breathe water and drown in air, which drastically changed my gameplay for the worse. I knew it was definitely the worst origin, with the most drawbacks, and that's why I chose it 😎. Dema chose one that let him phase through blocks at the cost of, like, 8,000,000 hunger and also burning in sunlight. I don't remember everyone elses, but I remember ours, because they had significant drawbacks, and thus changed the way we actually played the game.
