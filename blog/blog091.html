Divinity II: Developer's Cut is not good

I played Divinity Original Sin 2 last week and had an incredible, amazing time. I ended up killing a lot of people, in my playthrough, uh, because you can kill anyone, and the game accounts for that, so when I hit a roadblock I would just kill the roadblock if I didn't want to do their quest, and if I saw a guy murder an innocent person (like Lord Kemm) I would kill them and all dozen of their lackeys. So I missed out on a lot of content. I think that's fine — I love doing blind playthroughs where I'm not aware of all the things I'm missing out on, and am getting a unique experience that you can only have while blind — but boy howdy was it controversial in the Bencord. The most controversial thing I did was genocide all of Reaper's Coast after I was done with the area, for the XP and items, because there's literally no narrative downside to doing so. Some people in particular were extremely fucking mean to me, saying things like "I'm surprised someone like you made a whole game with character writing in it", "what I'm learning here is that I should not trust zug's opinions on video games", and "The consequences are unironically the stains upon your soul and conscious, be it literally or as part of roleplay". Um. So I feel like I should never talk about videogames with people ever again. It made me feel really bad about myself. And also it made me really mad, because the people who were chastizing me for playing it wrong had not themselves played DOS2. Like I'm still mad about it.

This is all to say I loved Divinity Original Sin 2. I played as Fane, which was really interesting, and I loved Beast and Malady. And then I found out about Divinity Fallen Heroes, and that it was cancelled, and that I'd never play the Malady video game. So. I decided to play Divinity II, which is an ARPG which was released in 2009 and which takes place 60 years after Divinity Original Sin 2 in the timeline (Divinity Original Sin 2 released in 2017).

So yeah. I bought it, and played about 6 hours of it. Uh, I very quickly realized it was not what I was expecting it to be. The world it takes place in is so fundamentally different to the world of DOS2 that it's basically just a different plane of existence but with a similar timeline and nouns. Which, you know, is fine, if the world was interesting. But it's just generic fantasy slop. You're a Dragon Hunter and need to hunt Dragons and also kill a lot of "Tribal Goblins" (😬), but then you turn into a Dragon Knight, and have to kill the Big Bad Enemy Guy, who watches over you and insults you and is like "haha my lackeys will take care of you, you're so insignificant". And the writing, the writing is directed so differently to the writing in DOS2, it's like completely tonally different and through a completely different authorial voice. Which, y'know, would be fine, if it was good. But it's not! It fucking sucks. Everyone speaks in references and it's cringe and embarrassing and constantly takes you out of the world. I decided to stop playing after I got to the second world and a guy went "I'm invincible! I am the walrus!" and it was so bad. It was so bad. I didn't want to see any more.

Oh, and the gameplay sucks. It's not a mid ARPG, it's a bad ARPG, with terrible balance, extremely unsatisfying movement, and very simple and boring combat. There is absolutely nothing interesting to it. And boy howdy, if you thought the Feats in Larian's CRPGs were "notoriously uninteresting and bad", imagine an ARPG where every upgrade and skill is even worse than those. It's so unsatisfying, it's so boring, and worst of all, it's so uninteresting.

The entire game is just mostly uninteresting. There is nothing to it that wasn't done better by its contemporaries or games made before it. Like I could be playing Drakengard instead. Or I could replay Morrowind for the nth time. There's just nothing to it.

I'm glad Larian is free from the D&D license and won't be touching Hasbro with a 10 foot pole because Jesus Christ DOS2 was so much better than BG3. It's like if BG3 had good combat and wayyyyyyyyyyy more interesting characters. Shame Fallen Heroes is forever cancelled. Malady is so fucking cool.
