Model Kits November-Early December 2024

November was a difficult month, and boy howdy, did I build model kits to get through it. Reviews below!

HGBF Amazing Strike Freedom: it's quite nice! The back bling's connectors are weird, and some of the joints sure are weak, but that can be said for a lot of HG Build Fighters kits. It's very pretty. The Vrabe's fin funnels cannot be attached as shown on the Amazing's art due to different connector types, so you gotta turn the clip connector sideways to properly fit the fin funnels, and it feels really bad. Dunno why they didn't change the design to account for that! And I dunno what else to say, lol.

HGUC Zeta (revive): pretty mediocre! Like, it's good, but apparently the UC 0088 Zeta is a lot better. The stickers for the wave rider are truly horrendous, it's so bad. I had the majority of this kit built from a while back, with the back bling/wave rider unbuilt, because it looks so unfun to build, and. It's not unfun, it's just, like, jeez. The color correcting stickers are rough. Posability is good though.

Orange Cat Industry Super Robot Heroes ExCreR Slash Coat: the worst thing I've built. It was a close competition, but the ExCreR beat out the MG Phenex in the "parts that don't fit together right" category by having small pegs that use soft plastic and, thus, disintegrate while you're fitting them. And using a rectangular peg for a circular hole twice! It holds together like a brick, unlike the MG Phenex, but building it is truly horrendously terrible, and I cannot recommend ever getting it. It sure is pretty, though.

MG Eclipse: one interesting chica! I kinda dig it in a really strange way. It's sharp and angular, and the build process is real interesting, and though long, is quite fun. The legs are... really bizarre, and I could never quite figure out how to make them pose nicely while normal. I ended up transforming them into their wave rider configuration, then just posing them like normal legs, making them look like big pointy sticks, which I really love! The articulation on this thing is weirdly limited, and things don't hold quite right, but I guess that's fine. It looks the part.

HGRG G-Self: lotta stickers. LOTTA stickers. I think the design is really nice, but it could really use the equivalent of a revive or an RG. Or an MG, though that's probably not happening anytime soon. It uses the slightly minty white plastic some UC kits use, and I'm gonna be honest, I'm not a fan on this kit! I think it'd look nicer with a bluer white or a pure white. The articulation is also pretty limited, and the ankle armor is genuinely horrendously put in, it hurts the kit a lot. And the things on the shoulders pop off constantly while posing this thing. It's frustrating! I wish the kit were better designed, the G-Self is an intriguing mobile suit and it just has not been given justice.

The next thing I'm building is the P-Bandai Gundam Stormbringer P.F., which I'm picking up from my aunt's this friday. Polycaps, here I come.
