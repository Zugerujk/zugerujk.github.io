The Vrabe standing tall!

Model Kits August-October 2024

I loooooove building model kits. Pictured above are the guys I built over the past 3 months. Reviews below!

1/100 Kotobukiya Tsugumori: it's... okay, with one huge glaring flaw that hurts the kit an immense amount. First off, the build is mostly fine. It's nothing to write home about, and it's nothing truly terrible. The Death Stranding Reverse Trike is the best Kotobukiya kit I've ever built, and this is probably a distant third (second being Tamotu). The plastic feels a little cheap, and the waterslides aren't great quality. It can pose okay. It's honestly pretty boring of a kit. Now, the GLARING flaw - its 3mm connector up the butt fuckin' shears plastic! It shore through my 3mm connector! The stresses just aren't up to snuff, at all. I put it in the footstool position because, goddamn, it killed that connector! WTF!

HGGT Atlas Gundam: pretty great. It's a very complex HG, and though a lot of that is stickers, a fair bit is just honest-to-god complex engineering. It's a joy to put together, and there's plenty to panel line, and posing it is. I mean, it could be better. The rail connectors feel weird and not great, and the stresses on the plastic felt kinda uncomfortable in my hands, like I was bending it the wrong way. And getting it to stand in a proper position on its own is pretty hard. But it's very pretty, and a white/black/yellow mobile suit looks really clean, and it's, just, like, nice!

HGBF Hi-𝜈 Vrabe: honestly wayyyyyy better than I was expecting. It's based on the plenty-older HG Hi-𝜈, and you can feel it with the joints, but, I mean. The plastic feels quite good-quality, and the thing holds itself mostly-okay, and the design, though polycap-heavy, is solid. Its biggest issue is the joints are NOT solid. The waist joint is one big ball joint (i think?) that separates a shitton, due to the amount of weight on the top part of the body. The wings are glorious, and I love the bird aesthetic that's been recontextualized and expanded upon from the original Hi-𝜈's design, but jeez louise. The joints! Argh, the joints. Also, it's got a butt action base connector point, and so because it's got so much weight from the wings, it sits on the connector point lopsided (similar to the RG Unicorn Full Armor and the RG Nu). But, uh, other than that? It's quite good. And gorgeous! I don't even mind the blue that much!

RG RX-78-2 Ver2.0: exactly as you've heard. This thing's been covered to death, and yeah, it's complex, and extremely posable, and very fun to build, and gorgeous. It's the first grandaddy I've built, and, uh. It's good. It's a simple design iterated upon to such a degree that it kinda feels overly-excessive in detail. But hey, that's what RGs are for!

The next things I'm building are the MG Eclipse and the Super Robot Heroes ExCreR Slash Coat... if USAGS would fulfill my order, already 😭. I've never had this issue with them before but this order is taking forever to fulfill!
