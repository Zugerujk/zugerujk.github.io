Finasteride 6 Month Update

It works!

Not much else to say. I've gotten a very slight amount of regrowth, which is cool, I wasn't expecting any. I've been taking it alongside using nizoral twice-weekly, and, yeah, it works. Absolutely zero side effects, though most side effects are things that I already have or that other medications already cause. I've gained about 8 pounds over the past 6 months (after almost no fluctuation in weight for about 5 or 6 years), but that's probably due to my mood being dimmed by events covered in other blog posts. Booyah! Don't gotta worry about balding for now.
