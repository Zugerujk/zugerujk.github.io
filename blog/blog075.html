I feel weird about writing Yo • to • Yo • ta

What the title says. As the personal crunch for releasing IOOD approaches I've been taking time off revising the writing by planning out and writing Yo • to • Yo • ta — the dream I had, the best work of art I've ever seen for those unacquainted. It's not even complicated — it's a complicated story, and I've been doing a lot of thinking about how best to tell it, but that's besides the point — the story is Mormon. The story is bleedingly Mormon, a Mormon tale about an ex-Mormon discovering that God is real through great personal turmoil and then joining the holy war on the side of Mormonism. And then Mormonism wins! Mormonism defeats the ultimate evil, which is defeated by it converting to Mormonism.

Like, why here, now? I'm an athiest, but it's not that I feel weird and bad about writing a story where a god is real and wins. Rather, it's that the state of the world is one being ruled by nationalistic christofascism. And, like, why use my voice, now, to write a story about a god which for all intents and purposes is a stand-in for the Mormon conception of god, being correct and winning? I hate the Mormon church. I'm obsessed with their mythology and way of life because it's insane and layered, but, they're unbelievably bigoted and anti-feminist and backwards and evil. And they hold immense political influence over a significant portion of the U.S., the place I live.

It feels weird, and bad. I've been escaping this escape recently by playing Pokeclicker alongside my friends Cleffa and Milo — it's singleplayer but we all began at the same time and are discovering small details and the "clicker meta" together. And fuck. I love Yo • to • Yo • ta, it's phenomenal! But the politics of it, it's uncritically, deeply backwards. And I'm bringing it into existence outside of my mind. In 2025.

It's hard to shake.
