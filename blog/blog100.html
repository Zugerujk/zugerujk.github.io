I'm a Gen Alpha ally

I was at Fawkes Alley this morning, the coffee shop right by Loring Park. I got my usual drink, a medium dark chocolate iced mocha (but with almond milk today instead of 2%), and a raspberry chocolate bread. It was alright. They're not the best mochas nor the best pastries but the environment is really bustling and bright and so I like going there every once in a while. And as I was sitting, I saw a kid with their parents holding a labubu coloring book.

I complimented the labubu coloring book. The kid had drawn the labubus well. They were swag. And then the parents were like "you know what labubu is, right? what are labubus?" and then I explained labubu to the parents, and how you can buy them on popmart's website, and how some are plush keychains and some are plastic figures. And how labubu were originally drawings and stuff. And then I finished my coffee, wished the family well, and left fawkes alley to join in the absolutely wonderful 60 degrees fahrenheit morning air.

I had left Fawkes Alley having done a great service to that child, and really the children of the world: I had told their parents how to buy them the thing they liked. That child will grow up having owned a labubu. And I'm proud to have done my part.
