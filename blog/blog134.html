I Read MrBeast's New Book ):

By now, a little over a week after its release, The Most Dangerous Games is infamously terrible across certain Internet circles. But I wanted to be in on the ground floor of this, the same way I was on the ground floor of Morbius. I've been doing research by watching MrBeast videos and he hasn't promoted it at all in anything recent of his — I think because he's ashamed of it in some way. It's a shameful book!

I bought it at the Tulsa International Airport, alongside a mysterious non-Feastables chocolate bar.

If you don't know anything about it, it's a knockoff of Squid Games, as is most of MrBeast's life. It's theoretically by MrBeast and James Patterson but it's not written by either of them. Whatever ghostwriter wrote this had significant input from Claude or ChatGPT, as you can hear its sour whisper throughout the entire novel. It's got an ARG element(?) where the reward at the end is $1,000,000 because MrBeast's only marketing tactic is giving away money. And it's a flop.

I just need to say I read it.

The Beast around another airport.

I finished it 6 days ago and I cannot remember the name of a single character from the book except MrBeast, who is frequently referred to by his full name James Donaldson (or maybe it was Jimmy Donaldson? I'm not picking the book back up, I'm writing this into a Discord text chat while at a friend's album release show at First Avenue 7th Street Entry). He's a virtuous, fearless action hero, replacing Gi-hun from Squid Game, whose only purpose is to be awesome and amazing and suuuuper cool and badass and to topple the evil business empire running the titular games, though I cannot remember the actual name of them.

I drove to a Caribou Coffee out in the suburbs to finish the Beastbook.

The prose is childish and the story is nonsensical as well as racist and sexist. Less said about it the better. They randomly go to a nondescript active war zone somewhere in South America and people speak in super racist written-out accents that are nonsensical and when the innocent child character is gravely injured the two female characters randomly surprise him with a kiss at the exact same time, causing him to blush.

MrBeast's channel is referenced in the book. One of the contestants references his bunker video which is one he seems particularly proud of. It's embarrassing.

Wow as I'm writing this out I'm kind of running out of things to say about this shitty book. It's just quite bad in an unentertaining way and only interesting in that MrBeast is attached to it and stars in it. You get the point. Worst book I've ever read. Was in contest with Dhalgren but Dhalgren is masterful between the parts that are the most racist thing you've ever read and Beastbook (as I took to calling it with my friends) just has absolutely nothing to it.

First band just started so good time to wrap this up. Book bad. Do not read. If you really really want to read it just pirate it or email me and I'll send you my copy.
