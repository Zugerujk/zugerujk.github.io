Spoilers, I ended up refunding it after 3.1 hours. First, it's clear this game was made for PS5. Uhhh, the menu design uses really small things, like clearly for a 1440p or 4k screen (I've got a 1080p screen). Also it's made for HDR, because, it is really flatly-lit in SDR. I have a dualsense that my mom bought me over the holidays and, uh, it's cool that it uses all the dualsense's features, though not super well. Idk. The only other PS5 games I've played on PC are Death Stranding Director's Cut and Returnal and, like, neither of those are really PS5 games, you know? You know what I mean. But yeah uh this is for PS5 and my computer set up is like PS4 level, so. It doesn't do good.

Like I had all the settings to their minimums and had all the gross Nvidia blurry dynamic resolution DLSS stuff that looks terrible but makes the game run better on and it still couldn't even hit a consistent 30.
