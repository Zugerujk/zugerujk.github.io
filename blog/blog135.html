Metaphor: ReFantazio — Good!

Nothing shocking. It's like Persona 5 but if it were good instead of bad. Squaring the circle.

I beat it. Thought it was alright. Well. I was playing it and then the game ended after a made a decision and then I uninstalled the game. The Persona series does "bad endings" a lot, and Metaphor has a few of them (though none of them in-depth in the slightest). There's one during the final boss fight, after More goes, like, "Tokyo is real, man", where you can accept reality. Idk. I did that. It was a bad ending. Then the game ended. Didn't get a miniature epilogue of the bad ending, didn't get to see a clip of Will doing karaoke after the credits — none of that! But I felt satisfied with the game and had my time with it, so, I just didn't go back and get the good ending. 🤷‍♀️

Craaaaazy how good the Press Turn system is.

Combat balancing could be a lot better. Heismay dodgetanking completely invalidates the endgame difficulty and the entire concept of Royal Archetypes kind of ruins the build crafting system they've created because everyone gets specced into one final Super Duper Ultra class. And there's absolutely no reason to not always use the Royal Archetypes, as they are so vastly better than everything else on a character. Such a fucking bummer! Especially, man, especially because I built a magic Will. The game incentivizes it: early game you are given multiple magic archetypes that other characters don't neatly slot into, and then immediately after the first dungeon (a sprawling P5-esque palace, truly the worst in the game), you're given a bevy of physical or supportive archetypes. And also your magic archetypes don't get their upper forms because it's all locked behind supports, and Gallica's support is story-locked. And then Will auto-unlocks the Prince Archetype and it's built for physical but also has a skill which gives you 4 extra turn icons (!!!), meaning you should literally never use it over anything else. And then you still unlock a bunch of archetypes after that, even as you're just grinding for Royal Archetypes for the rest of the characters.

And man Heismay dodgetanking is just. Fucking broken. You need an item to make it fully clear all of an enemy's press turn icons initially but for some reason the Royal Archetype turns that into a passive skill, making Heismay even more busted for literally no reason. Because in theory you've gotta sacrifice your item slot to cheese the game, which, you know, items aren't really powerful unless they're The Ones That Cheese The Game, but at least you've gotta sacrifice a portion of your build, you know? But not with Royal Archetypes, which are also just vastly better anyways. Idk.

The game could've been so much better gameplay-wise. Story-wise too. It drags at times. But it's good! They finally made a p5vania that isn't bad.

Story is generic as hell — what do you expect? — but man the gameplay is soooooo well-tuned.
