Magdalena Bay Imaginal Mystery Tour Fall 2024

I just saw Magdalena Bay, at First Avenue, in Minneapolis! It was amazing! I loved it! The opener was Valgur, and they were really interesting, and I liked them a lot. Outside, while waiting in line, I met two really cool guys, named Matt and Jackson, and then another really cool person, named Mossy. We talked a shitton, and I was next to Matt and Jackson during the show itself. It was great! They both work at Micro Center! They get commissions on purchases! Go to Micro Center St Louis Park Minnesota and buy some really expensive headphones!

Me in the pit, and me with the guys! My iPhone SE hated the lighting!

Mag Bay was impeccable. They were so good! They played all of Imaginal Disk, front to back, alongside a few other tracks. They played Dreamcatching, from Mercurial World, which I went HARD for, oh my GOD. I was going hard the entire fucking time, my legs hurt so fucking bad. They closed out the show with The Beginning (after The Ballad of Matt & Mica), and fucking, it was incredible! And Chaeri, from the Fall 2022 Mercurial World tour, made a cameo during the beginning of the show in the mirror! Idk! It was great! I loved it! Photos above! I don't know if I liked it as much as the Fall 2022 Mercurial World show in OKC — that shit was life changing — but it was still really phenomenal.
