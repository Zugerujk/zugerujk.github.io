Wishlists: An Analysis

The Steam page for Island Off Outer Darkness went live on April 17th, 2025, though I did not advertise it publicly until April 19th, after I had finished and uploaded the Steam trailer. And at that point in my life, I gained a new catchphrase: "wishlist my game on Steam". I've been saying it all the time.

The Steam page and announcement was posted in four different friend group servers, #playground and #auditorium in RFCK (Kip posted it in #auditorium and @everyone'd), the Bencord, my Bluesky, and my Twitter. I've advertised it to many in-person friends, as it's all I talk about, and after my last session of ketamine therapy my nurse asked about the game (because I've been talking to my psychiatrist about it, it's a very positive thing in my life), and I gave the whole spiel to her.

So, with this, 29 days later, how do the wishlists look?
