General Hospital Good, Hello Kitty Island Adventure Bad

I've replaced my Tubi slop addiction with watching General Hospital, the soap opera. I hopped on immediately after the 2025 Nurses Ball and I've been lightly hooked since. It's a lot healthier than Tubi slop. It's also fucking insane, I really do feel like everybody should hop on for an arc or two. One major event happens, and then the rest of that week's episodes are filled with flatly-shot single-location dialogue scenes between 2-4 characters, talking about that thing, or continuing background storylines. And it's so dramatic. And everybody talks in reference to The Thing That Just Happened and also That Thing That Happened Five Years Ago. And it's definitely self-aware too, sometimes a character will make a list of things that have happened to them and it's, like, amazing. My faves are Nina (Cynthia Watros), Marco (Adrian Anchondo), Alexis (Nancy Lee Grahn), Anna (Finola Hughes), Trina (Tabyana Ali), and Lois (Rena Sofer). Top two are Nina (Cynthia Watros) and Marco (Adrian Anchondo). And there's an episode every weekday. It's beautiful. And also ketamine is a plot point right now.

In contrast to this, man, Hello Kitty Island Adventure's writing is so bland. I'm in the lategame and it's ridiculous how every storyline is the same. Even events, which have new, specific dialogue, are all just "woah, thing happened. But thanks to friendship, everything is cool and good. Friendship makes me feel so good." When anything bad happens, it's very neutral, and it's because of nature or a lack of friendship or historical loneliness. And the solution is friendship. And everyone smiles.

Idk. It's so frictionless, and especially now, I'm not in the mood. 'Cuz idk if y'all have heard, but boy howdy, I'm doing fucking horrendously right now, my consistently-closest friend of the last 3.5 years cut contact with me on a probably-permanent timeframe. And every night it hits me like a nuclear missile and it's so large in scope I have no idea how to handle it. But I feel so much loneliness, and such emptiness, I can't exist in a future where they're not in it, but they're not, and it's my present. It overflows and breaks into, like, a null feeling. But the gameplay is good, y'know. Someone needs to make an Animal Crossing-like with basic plot beats like "someone's mistress gives birth while in divorce court while the person delivering the baby is the wife divorcing the husband because the ambulances can't get there in time." Imagine that! And then the husband gets the children!
