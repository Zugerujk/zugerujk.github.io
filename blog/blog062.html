I'm Balding

Not much, but it's advancing. I've been taking scalp photos intermittently over the past 2 years. And yeah, it's progressing. I didn't think to check the advance until my brother talked about himself balding, and though I'm 6 years younger, I've signs of early aging due to childhood cancer, so.

I thought I wouldn't care much. I was bald for a while, as a child, from chemotherapy and radiation. I like to say my hair is thinner and finer because of that, but honestly, I don't remember how my hair felt before. I don't remember much of anything before. My memory mostly begins after I had cancer.

It's like I've spent my entire life clawing away from the vacuuous all-consuming. I have cancer screenings every summer. They thought I had liver cancer for 2 days when I was a teenager due to me having globules on my liver where there's liver scarring from where my tumor was (and where my right kidney used to be, before it was killed by a tumor and then taken out). I had such intense anxiety I got a fever. 'Cuz it would've been a death sentence.

It's inevitable, really. I wanna start finasteride in order to stop the regression of my scalp, and I plan to, but. I'm gonna die. I've known it every day of my life for over half my life now. And now I look in the mirror and my scalp reminds me.
