SGDQ 2025 Part 1 (Bad)

Being at SGDQ 2025 has made me feel really weird and bad. There's this potent sense that I don't belong there, that it ain't my place. I felt that when I walked through Minneapolis Pride 2025, wrote about it in a whole never-published blogpost. I just don't feel like I belong anywhere. The only place I've ever felt belonging was RFCK and I'm reminded by that whenever I try belonging anywhere else. I am not of these places, these people; I'm an interloper on someone else's ground.

I just walked out of the Majora's Mask All Masks NMG run because I couldn't handle being in the room where it happens. I was looking forward to this one since Sunday, and idk, I just couldn't handle it. There were these two guys to the back and left of me, gossiping about GDQ, and volunteering, and heading the thing. And mistakes the management had willingly made in past years, and their bad experiences (something about someone who does the announcing being at the event but filling an away spot?). And that's fine. I just couldn't handle it. All I could think about was my time working on RFCK, my experience with and within management. Just completely banal commonplace failures. Everything reminds me of it. It haunts me.

But yeah, you know, I'm at GDQ, it's fine. I wish I could enjoy it more. I'm trying, I'm really fucking trying. I don't know anybody and everybody already knows everybody. I've tried striking up conversation like twice in two perfectly-applicable moments and it's a total fucking failure. I met a guy named Nicholas but we just played some arcade games together, and then I saw them today, and we didn't do anything. Which is fine.

I realized while writing notes today that my Splatoon 3 Big Swig Roller charm fucking fell off my lanyard. So it's gone. "Can't have anything to myself, even things that suck", I wrote, for Eis, the character. Things I wrote for him have constantly been popping up in my mind the past three days. "But never so, never with me!", other self-centered bullshit I made to animate my self-insert who ruined his personal life and can never be happy, which now haunts my own mind, as I ruined my personal life and feel like I can never be happy. I had him quote dog thoughts by Anna Haifisch, "here's the life I've always longed for". God I'm so fucking lonely and depressed.

It's hard to say "I'm glad I tried it out at least" because I'm trying but I feel like it'd be better to live with the vague discomfort at having never tried and also the unspent $200. Billie Eilish "think I forgot how to be happy" blazing in my mind right now. Thank god it's not something I wrote.

All three days now I've just tried going there and having a good time and every time I leave midday, unhappy, depressed, feeling like shit. I'm my own worst enemy, my inability to merge with the crowd and make joy and be happy. Maybe I'll try harder tomorrow. There's still four days to go. I don't know. I feel like I'll always be the person Kip and LIAM said I was in a podcast four years ago, because that's who I've always been. Awkward, lonely, out-of-touch, annoying. I'm a creep, I'm a weirdo.
