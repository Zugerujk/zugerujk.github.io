I can feel the end of IOOD, now

I've been working on Island Off Outer Darkness for a year and three months now. I'm in the final stages of the end of development. And this morning I've had such a calm wash over me. I've had so many spinning plates in the air for so long, but, I can feel it. I can feel the end of development approaching. When I can stop worrying, and stop having to work all the time. Putting a deadline down has been so constricting and also so freeing. Because now I can really feel this.

I'm so grateful for this.
